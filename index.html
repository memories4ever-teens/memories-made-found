Our story

A little support can make a lasting difference.

Memories Made Foundation was inspired by a simple belief: a student's financial circumstances should not decide whether they get to be part of the experiences that shape high school.

What began with community members coming together to help students attend prom grew into a broader vision — supporting students across North Central Massachusetts so they can participate in the activities, traditions, and shared experiences that help make their school years meaningful.

Because the memories made in high school should be about the experience — not whether a student could afford to be there.
Student support

More chances to say “I'm in.”

We work toward reducing financial barriers that can keep students from participating in extracurricular activities and school experiences.

For students & families

If cost is standing between a student and an activity, reach out to learn about available support and the process for requesting assistance.

For schools & educators

School staff can help identify opportunities where financial assistance could make participation possible for a student.

For our community

Donors, local businesses, and volunteers help turn community generosity into real opportunities for students.

Support is subject to available funding and foundation guidelines. Please contact the foundation for current eligibility details and application procedures.
